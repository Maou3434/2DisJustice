# Cinematic & Editorial Portfolio Art Direction

## Core Objective
Deliver deeply designed, dimensional, and narrative-driven digital portfolio experiences that reject generic template tropes and convey commanding engineering and design maturity.

---

## Variation B: Ghost of Tsushima / Feudal Japanese Aesthetic System

### 1. Conceptual Pillars
- **Ma (間) — Negative Space**: Emptiness is not absence; it is breathing room and pregnant tension. Generous, asymmetric margins and intentional pauses between narrative beats.
- **Wabi-Sabi (侘寂)**: Beauty in restraint, natural textures, and weathered materials (charred cedar/shou sugi ban, wet stone, handmade washi paper, folded steel).
- **Kaze (風) — Environmental Movement**: Subdued environmental motion (drifting fog particles, directional wind streaks, falling ginkgo/maple leaves, ink dissipation) that reacts subtly to user scroll.
- **Shodo (書道) — Calligraphic Energy**: Expressive brush gestures, vertical typographic accents, stamped seals (inkan/hanko), and crisp razor-sharp cuts.

### 2. Color Palette & Materiality
- **Sumi Black (墨)**: `#0D0E11` to `#16181D` (deep soot ink, not harsh synthetic `#000000`).
- **Washi Cream / Raw Silk**: `#F4F0E8` to `#E6DFC8` (soft, tactile contrast for typography and brushstrokes).
- **Cinnabar / Vermilion (朱色)**: `#C83226` or `#B8281D` (strict, singular accent for seal stamps, critical calls to action, and focal points).
- **Weathered Slate & Moss**: `#2A3038` and `#3E463A` (subdued midtones for technical specs and card substrates).

### 3. Spatial & 3D Integration
- **Atmospheric Canvas**: Layered canvas backdrop with subtle procedural mist/fog shaders or drifting particulate wind.
- **Hero Artifact**: A tangible 3D or high-fidelity dimensional object (e.g. an interactive 3D Katana blade / tsuba guard, a weathered stone monument inscribed with code, or an unfolding scroll).
- **Sound & Haptics (Optional/Restrained)**: Subtle acoustic katana draw or wind chime on key milestone interactions, strictly user-muted by default.

## Variation A: Architectural / Experimental Editorial System

### 1. Conceptual Pillars
- **Editorial Typography**: Pairing an assertive display serif or architectural sans with a precise, high-contrast monospace technical label.
- **Dimensional Layering**: Deep z-plane hierarchy with overlapping typographic slabs, viewport-breaking project displays, and interactive spatial galleries.
- **Constructivist Layout**: Broken grids, asymmetric split screens, and deliberate rhythmic variation between high-density project matrices and vast typographic statements.

### 2. Palette & Surface
- **Monochrome Foundation**: Rich charcoal, bone white, cold titanium gray.
- **Electric Precision Accent**: One laser-focused accent (e.g. safety orange `#FF4F00`, signal amber `#F59E0B`, or cobalt blue `#1D4ED8`) used with extreme restraint.
- **Raw Physical Surfaces**: Perforated steel grids, glass prism refraction, blueprint wireframes, and architectural schematics.

## Content & Proof Strategy
1. **The Architecture First**: Don't just show screenshots; reveal the engineering challenge, the system topology, and the technical decisions made.
2. **Interactive Proof**: Live interactive widgets, live WebGL experiments, benchmarks, or runnable sandboxes instead of vague descriptions.
3. **True Provenance**: Never use fake logos or testimonials. Highlight genuine engineering passion, open-source work, and system capabilities.
